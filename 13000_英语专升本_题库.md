## 阅读理解（真题精选）

**Passage 1**  
The Internet has changed the way people communicate. Before the Internet, people relied on letters and telephone calls. Now, email and social media have made communication faster and more convenient. However, some people argue that face-to-face communication is still the most effective way to build relationships.

**1. What is the main idea of the passage?**  
A. The Internet is better than telephone  B. The Internet has changed communication  C. Letters are no longer used  D. Social media is dangerous  
✅ B。

**2. According to the passage, what do some people believe?**  
A. The Internet is useless  B. Email is too slow  C. Face-to-face communication is still best  D. Letters are better than email  
✅ C。

---

**Passage 2**  
Global warming is one of the most serious environmental problems facing the world today. The average temperature of the Earth has increased by about 1°C over the past century. Scientists believe that human activities, especially the burning of fossil fuels, are the main cause of this warming.

**3. What is the main cause of global warming according to scientists?**  
A. Natural disasters  B. Human activities  C. Animal extinction  D. Volcanic eruptions  
✅ B。Human activities, especially burning fossil fuels。

**4. How much has the Earth's temperature increased over the past 100 years?**  
A. 5°C  B. 3°C  C. About 1°C  D. 10°C  
✅ C。

## 词汇与语法

**5.（单选）He ___ to school by bus every day.**  
A. go  B. goes  C. going  D. gone  
✅ B。第三人称单数，用goes。

**6.（单选）She ___ her homework when I called her.**  
A. does  B. did  C. was doing  D. has done  
✅ C。过去进行时——我打电话的时候她正在做作业。

**7.（单选）This is the book ___ I bought yesterday.**  
A. who  B. what  C. which  D. where  
✅ C。定语从句，先行词book是物，用which。

**8.（单选）I don't know ___ he will come or not.**  
A. if  B. that  C. whether  D. what  
✅ C。whether...or not固定搭配。

**9.（单选）He is ___ of the two brothers.**  
A. tall  B. taller  C. the taller  D. tallest  
✅ C。两者比较用the+比较级。

**10.（单选）The problem is ___ difficult that nobody can solve it.**  
A. so  B. very  C. too  D. such  
✅ A。so...that句型。

## 写作范文

**题目：环境保护（Environmental Protection）**

Nowadays, environmental protection has become increasingly important. With the rapid development of industry, our environment is facing serious challenges such as air pollution and water pollution.

There are several ways to protect the environment. First, we should reduce the use of plastic bags. Second, we can save energy by turning off lights when leaving a room. Last but not least, the government should make stricter laws to control pollution.

In my opinion, everyone should play a part in protecting the environment. Only in this way can we create a better world for future generations.

**万能句型（直接背）：**
- Nowadays, ... has become increasingly important.
- There are several reasons for this. First, ... Second, ... Last but not least, ...
- In my opinion, ... Only in this way can we ...
